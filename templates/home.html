End-to-End Factory Quality Workflow

The 4-stage operational pipeline mandated for zero-defect dispatch compliance

All 4 Core Features Live
01 Feature 1
Role & Job Setup

Production jobs are created with exact nominal dimensions, upper tolerances, and lower tolerances. Role-guarded logins ensure inspectors, supervisors, and admins only perform authorized tasks.

02 Feature 2
Metrology Inspection

Inspectors record physical measurements for sample units. The built-in tolerance engine calculates real-time PASS/FAIL. If out-of-tolerance, the system strictly enforces attachment of ≥ 2 defect photos.

03 Feature 3
Supervisor Clearance

Supervisors evaluate pending inspection batches. They can Approve, Reject, or Return for re-measurement with required remarks. Anti-self-approval rules prevent inspectors from rubber-stamping their own work.

04 Feature 4
Dispatch Gatekeeper

Dispatches are evaluated against a real-time 4-rule checklist (batch quantity, approval status, zero unresolved holds, within tolerance). Releases generate a tamper-evident Certificate of Analysis (COA).

Official Certificate of Analysis (COA) Engine

QualiTrack features a dedicated ReportLab PDF generator and print stylesheet designed for factory floor compliance. Every cleared batch receives a cryptographic SHA-256 seal, batch traceability number, sign-off authorization, and printable gatepass certificate with zero layout bugs or orphan pages.
